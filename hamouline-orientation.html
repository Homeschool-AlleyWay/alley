Tour schedule

Suggested tour time: 30 minutes, about five minutes per stop. Families can pause and return. The full platform will track the 10-school-day deadline from enrollment; this preview does not enforce deadlines.

Teacher introductions & academic placement

After enrollment, families will receive a grade-level assessment, recommended courses, and teacher introductions describing the next two weeks. At least two priority recommendations will be selected before the final schedule is approved.

Not connected yet: assessment scoring, actual teacher videos, counselor booking, account-based completion, and school-day deadline enforcement.
